<objective>
Contain the ACCEPT ambiguity — the phase's named regression gate — and prove containment against
both corpora.

This is the only place in the phase where standard COBOL is at risk. `_accept_body`
(grammar.js:1513) already ends with `seq($._FROM, field('from', choice(…, $.WORD)))`, so

character of IDMS grammar is added. The IDMS form diverges only on the token that follows. 1,757
standard occurrences sit behind that rule against 634 IDMS ones, and the resolution of this
shift/reduce decision is exactly where a silent reclassification of ordinary COBOL would hide.

Output: a FORK-03-separable grammar commit series plus a recorded differential result on both
corpora.

<flagged_assumptions>
- **IDMS-03's `unclassified` edge (raised in 02-02, still unresolved).** The differ inventories node
  type, position and a cleanliness qualifier — it does **not** inventory subtrees. A standard
  `accept_statement` whose node type is unchanged but whose *children* changed (for example a
  `from:` field now resolving to a different alternative of the `_accept_body` choice) would pass
  the gate silently. Closing it would mean inventorying full subtrees over 52,233 files, a
  materially different cost. Surface the residual explicitly when reporting the differential
  result; do not describe the gate as proving more than it proves.

- **RESEARCH assumption A2 (`ACCEPT DB-KEY RELATIVE TO CURRENCY`).** The exact operand-tail shape of
  the `RELATIVE TO` variant was not independently re-verified against its dedicated manual page;
  only the anchor keyword's presence was confirmed. If that variant's trailing keyword differs from
  a bare `DB-KEY` anchor, D-06's anchor set could under-match. The differential surfaces it
  empirically as a CONVERTED shortfall against 634 — that shortfall is the evidence D-06 requires
  before extending the anchor set, and extending it stays inside this phase.

- **`idms_unparsed_tail` does NOT fire for a missed IDMS ACCEPT (D-06).** An unanchored IDMS ACCEPT
  form falls back to the standard `accept_statement` and ERRORs on its tail. For ACCEPT
  specifically the coverage signal is the CONVERTED count landing short of 634 — not a tail
  census. Do not read a zero tail count for ACCEPT as full coverage.
</flagged_assumptions>
